now then, what is LOD? Level of detail, pretty self explanatory, it is the level of detail, but what does it actually refer to? so for this, ill first go into video game optimization dun dun dun!

so, in video games, we have assets, which are all the things u see in the game ranging from the street to the bench to the car to the treasure chest u open in fortnite and go wild over when its a golden spazz, all dat shi, is assets. and most of the time, assets are detailed as fuck which makes them, heavier, and more expensive on the gpu to render aka show on ur screen. so one technique used to balance the LOD, or level of detail, is to use a balanced approach, mainly, the things closer to your character, or near you on the screen, are rendered, in higher detail, while the futher something is from you, it is rendered in lower detail. and most of the time, you will never notice this unless you are specifically trying to hunt for this. In every game nowadays, as u move around the game world, the lower detailed assets, get replaced with higher detailed ones, so u always feel like the game is so smexxy waow. but in reality, it is but a well made illusion, like most things in life, another concept u should def check out :d the illusion of life :d (ion think ive written it yet lol)


So thats a very basic tldr of what level of detail or lod means in the video game industry. u have a tree, u make 3 versions of the tree, detailed one for very close player, a medium one for when its not too far away, and then one version for the furthest away. and these can be things like the closest version having insane realistic wooden details u can see the grains and branches and leaves and maybe even birds or nests etc, while the medium one is just the same shape of the tree + basic colors not many details, maybe even reduced branches and instead of having every leaf be detailed its just one big bush like thingi. which from afar, gives the illusion of being a normal tree. and then for the lowest furthest, u could literally replace the 3d asset with a 2d image of a tree which rotates itself to face the viewer. some games which are meant to run on even the lowest tier of devices, dont even use 3d assets, its all just images coz theyre inexpensive to render, tankionline, if any are familiar, is one such game, the tanks are just 3d cubes, that float, the trees and bushes are just 2d images u can phase through, the skye is one image, the map itself is one tiny square. because its meant to run on the lowest tier of devices, the game gave up on detail and instead went for accessibility + speed. despite the looks, it is a fun game i have spent my childhood playing and still do tho rarely. ah man claudio and the gng doing trickshots was insane, def checkout tanki online trickshot vids, what a time man. 

now, that last bit about the  tanki online game might seem out of place, but its not. here id like to point out, that the same concept is also used in art, in a multitude of ways. first lets take the tanki online example. low af detail + speed which still makes the game fun because the core loop is not designed around fancy graphics. similarly, if you are someone who reads manga, you would know that in the world of manga, while every mangaka is no doubt insane, they all have, broadly speaking, different levels of details made to fit their manga. most weekly manga chapters focus on speed over detail, while on the opposite end, monthly mangas, feature much more detailed art in comparison. ofc, there are crazy ass mofos who do detailed af art while doing weekly chapters and i have nothing but respect for them, and then we also have the opposite with monthly ones, still going for little detail. so while this isnt directly LOD as per art, which ill break down, u can kind of see that the core idea behind LOD is to optimise the work at hand, for its purpose, audience, or whatever constraints you have.
