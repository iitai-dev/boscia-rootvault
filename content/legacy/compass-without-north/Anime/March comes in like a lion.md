bro i know alot of people said this was like a philosophiocal psychological show and shi and man it is that but its different. its the most unique anime ive seen in terms of how it portrays the main characters life. mostof the time we have a short scene and the mc with his thoughts. alot of it is like self thinking or reflection and its refreshing to see. the mc ye maybe a bit dumb coz hes literally a highscholer with no friends so people may not like it, but as someone who can kinda relate not alot just a lil bit, it hits home. i love the realism aspect, the story is nice, and heartwarming yet sad. its weirdly addicting, ive just been sitting and watching episode after episode, forcibly stopping myself from watching more as i want to enjoy it. some episodes are dark, some are sad, some are happy, some are everything. the artstyle is hella nice, the soft colour palette and the way the eyes are drawn is hella unique.

### episode 10 
had that scene with the enemy dude being a drunk when he loses and has fightswith his family, and rei well has to beat him. the scene with rei running and shouting was refreshing, i dont think many series would show this aspect. i was fully expecting him to go and cheer up the dude or try to fix things but then i realised, thats not his problem nor his fault. he needs to win and fk ye he feels bad but at the end of the day, its not his fault. evryone is there to fight and if u feel that bad about losing GO PRACTICE. this bit was insane. purely insane speech.


### episode 12
had that scene where rei ran outta his home while sitting because he could feel something coming in his head. i understand that feeling man and the scene resonated with me so much, like to avoid thinking what u know u ca think, u do everything to avoid it. the whole scene with the music and the shots was insane. 

Episode 13 the scene with the enemy dude fking up rei, without him even realising was peak. like the way when rei realised he fked up he turned up from the board and looked at the guy he said, you finally looked up. like gahdamn. also the dude is like a big bro figure damn.


Season 2 episode 3
bro that scene with hina in the bath is so good. no i dont mean sexy or anything like that smh. i mean the rawness and emotion protrayed through that scene. the way everything is dark, just the moonlight and how shes simply staring into nothing or well the moon but here eyes are lost in thought. hella relatable coz i used to space out like that in the tub, not anymore tho coz i aint got no tub now pain. but ye insane portrayal of emotions. so good.
