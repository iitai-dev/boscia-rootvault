Bro this indie anime is insane. like sure its indie and the story at times feels pretty off, like theyd just be joking around before a fight, which i find hilarious at times but sometimes its a bit off, also alof othe fights end very very quickly, BUT SOME OF THEM HAVE INSANE CAMER AANGLES AND FRAMERATE STUFF AND OTHER STFF TOO. its insane man. I love the ED song, like the animation and song, they go well together like very well, soft vibes, all that and the animation scenes, like illustrations. peak. Also in the OP as well, there are some scenes which are very very good. the person who made this, i mean the artist duo i think, they have to be insane, i need to research them more. i love this art style, because its like cartoony but like insane compositions and effects and vibe, it all makes it look absolute peak. 

Also, its insane how cool the character designs are, like this has to be such a unique show where every character is unique, the mecha udes too, AND THE BEST PART IS THE THE DIFFERENT FORMS FIST TAKES WHEN HE TAKES OVER OTHER PEOPLE. also almas final form and stuff. insane man. theres alot of scenes in the fights and other too, which straight up just feel like full illustrations, i think thats possible due to the artstyle but thats such a cool thing, usually you dont get that vibe.

Lastly, the story is actually pretty good for something this short even with the issues it has. alot of different sub plots, the twists and character interactions. lowkey, i could use this is [[EDR]] inspiration. fking lit.

Like episode 11 was peak in the how they try to revive amatsuga, that whole sequence, peak shit. He was like, i have no emotions, no dreams, but i still wanna reach out.

Watching episode 12 and did bro just say yakumo and the others didnt die in an accident BUT WERE KILLED BECAUSE OF OTHER DIIOTS. this series really divesinto human foolishness and other aspects of human nature, shit ones, good ones. wiht the whole, doing this for greed, controlling mecha ude, manipulating everyone etc etc. insane. alot of people ar probably gonna miss the point but yeah, peak stuff.
the villains always have the purest reasons for doing wrong. theyre just trying to help the world, because they know changes require difficult decisions, BUT SOME FKING IDIOT WITH THE POWER OF FRIENDSHIP!

ALSO EPISODE 12 IS PURE INSANE ACTION SEQUENCE, COULD DEF USE FOR INSPO ESPECIALLY FOR MID AIR FIGHTS WITH WINGS AND MECH STUFF.

The ending was good. the way they actually find a way back, try to co-exist with them and all. it really made it feel like a full whole ending, with even potential for a new season but its ok. it ended. ive been searching for the original artist but i still havent been able to find them. i really wanna look at some original illustrations which inspired this whole thing and even the other illustrations, like the illustrations used in the anime for op and ed, and in general too, they were so fking cooool. i wanna see more but rarely anyone made art on it sad. maybe ill make some :p
