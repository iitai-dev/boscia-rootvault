Jellyfish can't swim at night is such a good fking show man. I love the characters their vibes and just how they go about life etc. The artstyle is vibey af and the way they show the mv as the Ed is insane. Hella relatable and I really wish I could start something like that. Man. 
-my thoughts after finding out about this anime

in general though i love the fking op, the feeling of being stuck and not fitting in. and the animation at some points is insane.
