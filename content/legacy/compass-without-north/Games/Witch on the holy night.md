# Main Game
man i loved the whole visual novel. it was like very chill and easy going and light hearted. the art and animation were insane, it felt even better than the tsuki remake at times. i loved all the characters and it didnt feel like there were any bits that felt useless, it was a solid visual novel. i loved the little interactions between characters, the little jokes and especially yhe expressions, like aokos mad expression and alices blank expression man. it was fun stuff. the story wasnt anything tooo insane but it was good. instead of like tsuki re where i was anticipating the next thing and trying to see which endings i would reach and stuff, i found myself to be simply enjoying this one, just slowly laughing and smiling at bits. id def love to replay this after, def gonna buy it on steam too.
